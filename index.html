Play every hand right. Then keep the count.

Five drills, in the order card counters learn them. Basic strategy comes first, because a perfect count is worth nothing if you misplay a 16.

Start with strategy

    Counting cards in your head is legal, but casinos can refuse your play. A skilled counter's edge is about 1% at best and the swings are large.

    Basic strategy

    One correct play for every hand against every dealer card. Learn it one rule at a time, then drill until the answer arrives before you have to think.

    Running count

    Hi-Lo gives every card a value. Add them as they land. A high count means the shoe is rich in tens and aces, and that favors you.

    True count and bets

    A running count of +6 means a lot with one deck left and little with five. Read the discard tray, work out the decks still in the shoe, divide, and round down. Then size the bet.

    The bet ramp

    Each point of true count is worth about half a percent. The house starts roughly half a percent ahead, so you are about even at +1 and ahead from +2. Bet the minimum until then.

    Live table

    You have the last seat, so every other hand plays out before yours. Count everything, bet with the ramp, and play the chart. The trainer flags each slip as it happens.
